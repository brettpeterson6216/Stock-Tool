      How it works

      Four questions, every company.

      1. 1What does the business actually do?

        Price, revenue, margins, cash flow, debt and dilution, each with its source and date.

      2. 2What is the price already assuming?

        LensScore and reverse valuation show the growth the current price needs to be right.

      3. 3What has to happen from here?

        Build Bear, Base and Bull cases. Reported facts and your own assumptions stay clearly separate.

      4. 4What would change your mind?

        Save the thesis, the downside and a review trigger, so next quarter you can check it, not guess.

      Questions

      Before you sign up

      I have never bought a stock. Is this for me?

      Yes. Start with the free lessons, then open any company and follow the same four questions. Every metric has a plain-English explanation one click away, and the free plan is not a trial: it does not expire.

      Does ImpliedLens tell me what to buy?

      No. It shows you the evidence, what the price implies and where the risks are, so you can make and defend your own decision. Nothing here is a buy or sell recommendation or personal financial advice.

      Where does the data come from?

      Financial statements come from the company’s own SEC filings (EDGAR XBRL). Prices and market data come from {{MARKET_PROVIDER_NAMES}}. Every figure shows its source and as-of date, and missing data is shown as missing rather than estimated.

      How is this different from TradingView or a stock-picking newsletter?

      TradingView is built around charts; newsletters hand you picks. ImpliedLens connects the chart to the business underneath it and to a valuation you can edit, so you understand why a stock might work, not just that someone likes it.
