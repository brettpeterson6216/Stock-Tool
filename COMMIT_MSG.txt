Rebuild the interface around a premium dark-first design system

The product read as flat outlined boxes on a pale surface: 3px radii, hairline
borders, no elevation, and twelve stacked stylesheets pulling in different
directions. This adds one authoritative layer that redefines the canonical
--il-* tokens every older sheet already maps onto, so the whole surface moves
together instead of component by component.

- Dark is now the default surface; light is rebuilt as warm paper.
- Cool ink surfaces layered by elevation, with warm gold as the sole accent.
- Radii 3px -> 10-28px, gradient sheens, lit top edges, real shadows.
- Instrument Serif for display type; DM Mono with tabular numerals for figures.
- Glass navigation, brushed-gold primary controls, inset-depth inputs.
- Ambient horizon light, fine grid, and grain so dark areas are not flat voids.
- Restrained scroll reveal and pointer parallax, with a reveal failsafe and a
  beforeprint handler so content can never stay hidden.

Also fixes three defects found along the way:

- Static pages bootstrapped the theme from an inline script, which the
  production CSP (scriptSrc 'self') silently blocked, so dark mode never
  applied on About, Blog, and the legal pages. They now load
  theme-bootstrap.js externally, which also satisfies the no-inline-scripts
  rule in the architecture doc.
- Primary calls to action rendered light gold text on a gold fill and were
  effectively unreadable, including the navigation trial button.
- The skip link was partially visible in the top-left corner while unfocused.

No feature, route, API, or markup logic was removed. Reverting is one <link>.
