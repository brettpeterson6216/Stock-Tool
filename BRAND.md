# ImpliedLens Brand Playbook

## Position

ImpliedLens is the decision workspace for self-directed investors who want to research the business, test the thesis, and revisit the decision.

## Promise

Transparent data. Reviewable models. Decisions you can revisit.

## Proof

- Provider-labeled market data and direct SEC filing links
- User-controlled valuation and scenario assumptions
- Connected thesis, risk, call research, watchlist, and portfolio workflows
- Honest limitations and visible methodology

## Voice

- Calm, specific, and evidence-led
- Explain uncertainty without sounding indecisive
- Prefer "latest available" to "real-time" unless the feed is verified real-time
- Prefer "model" or "scenario" to "prediction"
- Never imply guaranteed returns, secret signals, or automatic buy/sell answers

## Visual Direction

- Distinct deep gold: trust, judgment, and focus
- Neutral light and dark surfaces: research should remain the visual priority
- Green and red only for directional data
- Dense, legible information with restrained motion and minimal decoration

## Social Content Pillars

1. **One chart, one decision**: show a chart and explain the question it helps answer.
2. **Model audit**: demonstrate how one assumption changes a valuation range.
3. **Filing evidence**: link a primary filing and explain the useful detail.
4. **Call review**: summarize what changed versus the prior expectation.
5. **Process over prediction**: teach a repeatable research habit.
6. **Build in public**: show product improvements and the reasoning behind them.

## Weekly Publishing Cadence

- Monday: market context or one-chart post
- Tuesday: Academy framework
- Wednesday: product workflow demonstration
- Thursday: filing or earnings-call evidence
- Friday: model audit or scenario comparison

## Post Template

**Hook:** State the investor question.

**Evidence:** Show the specific chart, filing, metric, or assumption.

**Interpretation:** Explain what the evidence supports and what it does not prove.

**Next check:** Name the future evidence that would change the conclusion.

**CTA:** Link to the relevant ImpliedLens tool or ticker page.

## Claims Guardrails

- Do not fabricate reviews, user counts, returns, or case studies.
- Do not call third-party data proprietary.
- Do not call estimates reported facts.
- Disclose hypothetical assumptions and educational intent.
- Encourage honest reviews regardless of sentiment.
